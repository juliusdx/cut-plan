The honest directive

Maintenance block, 8 weeks (Oct–Dec). Rebuild strength and sleep. Same 4-day structure, fresh exercise variations, eat at maintenance. Then decide on a final cut with clean data.

Why maintenance: lifts flat or slipping, sleep still poor, waist stuck at 32" since July. The deficit stopped paying for itself. Goal now: lifts climbing again, sleep better, waist holding at 32" or slightly lower. Weight may rise 1–2 kg (glycogen + water) — expected.
Progression rule (double progression): pick a weight. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add 2.5 kg next session and drop back to the bottom of the range. Log every ★ lift — that's how we'll see whether strength is coming back.
One non-negotiable cardio: Norwegian 4×4 on the rower, once a week (Wed). Best value for heart health and VO₂max. (HbA1c back to 5.5% in Oct — resolved.)
Train closer to failure: with volume trimmed, every set matters. Aim for the last 2 reps of each set to be genuinely hard (1–2 reps in reserve). Junk volume past ~15 sets per muscle per week adds fatigue without growth — quality beats quantity here.
Hybrid structure: Main compound lifts (squat, bench, RDL, row) are done alone with full rest. Accessories pair non-competing muscles. Each session ~50–60 min instead of 75+. Faster sessions, better recovery, same stimulus.
Sleep basics: same wake time daily (weekends too) · no caffeine after 12pm · 4×4 / lifting done ≥3h before bed · last big meal 2–3h before bed with some carbs · room cool + dark · phone out of the bedroom.
Schedule: Mon Upper A · Tue Legs (only leg day) · Wed 4×4 · Thu Upper B · Fri Zone 2 + abs · Sat optional easy swim/walk + Stack A. Sunday pickleball/rest. Review: if squat drops >10% in 6 weeks, add sets back. Skip a day = no catch-up, just resume.
